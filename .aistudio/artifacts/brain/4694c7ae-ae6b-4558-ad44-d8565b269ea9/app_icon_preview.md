# Project Graffiti — App Icon Design Preview

Here is the design concept for **Project Graffiti**:

![Project Graffiti App Icon](/app/src/main/res/drawable/project_graffiti_icon_1791226501150.jpg)

### **Design Highlights:**
- **Stylized Graffiti 'G':** Bold, dynamic street art geometry with sharp flare cuts and fluid curves.
- **Vibrant Neon Palette:** Electric hot pink, bright street cyan, and golden sunshine spray highlights.
- **Thick Contrast Outline:** Crisp dark contours ensuring high visibility against both light and dark launcher themes.
- **App Store Ready:** Clean dark matte background with subtle aerosol glow, optimized for standard 108dp adaptive launcher icon masks (circle, squircle, rounded square).
